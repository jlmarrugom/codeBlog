Recently on March 20th I got to know that a close cousin of mine will be dad. My sister, my other cousins and I were Shocked! He's just 24 years old (1 year older than me). This fact put me in Philosophical mode, now I'm wondering an absurd amount of things, and this Journal entry is kind of a relief to my thoughts.

## Plans for the future

How can someone with just 24 years old be so relaxed when a child is coming his way? I think It is the cleverest thing to do. He could choose to be desperate, angry or Sad, but it only will make things harder. Being relaxed is not just something he must do, it is something we all should do when things like this come up, it demonstrates maturity, focus and a growth mindset. Relaxation will help us in difficult situations, meditate about our current state and what we must do, is what guarantees a positive outcome.

## Life with that decision and others (work, study, marriage)

I come to realize that having a child is just another decision in life, there are a lot of them that drastically change all of our future, it's just that we don't see them as important as having a child. Select a job offer over others, study abroad or study in your home country, keep studying or start working, marriage or not, these are decisions not as big as having a child, each of them change our future drastically, but we don't feel it that way. 

I made this exercise with myself today: Imagine you are watching a movie, where the main character is yourself, he or she is doing exactly the same thing and is in the same situation as you, maybe working on a company, thinking about formalize the relationship with our partner, apply for a scholarship on Europe, all of these like every other person that have to decide the exact same thing... How do you feel? Are you living the dream? Are your decisions the more exciting ones? Will those decisions make your life the life that you wanted 3, 5 or 10 years ago? 

Independently of your answers, in the future, Won't you be happy with what you choose? Our brains have a neuronal circuit that makes us feel Ok with our lives, it doesn't matter how many good choices you have made, or how many wrong ones, your Ok percentage usually goes from 50% to 100% if we are grateful enough. If not, you could try one of the alternatives listed below.

## Life with other decisions out of the box

What is life? Life is just a moment in the cosmos, a tiny fraction of a fraction on the universe calendar. It's common to think that we should make this moment worthy of living it, and is true, we should make every second count, and maybe living a "normal" life isn't just enough. But, What are the alternatives? Well, you can choose to: 

### Dedicate exclusively to your passion

Leaving everything else aside, you will go up the hill of mastery of your craft, you will focus on being the greatest programmer, the greatest artist, the greatest Esports player, the greatest standard in the discipline you choose. This way of living can be either lonely or full of friends depending on your approach, I recommend the full of friends path because loneliness could be painful sometimes, but, if you are choosing this way, you are probably an outlier, you could replace your social network with a deeper mastery of your work, this way you will achieve Superhuman highness and probably the work you'll end up doing will be as awesome as the biggest in history, you'll be remember at least a hundred years more than the rest of us.

### Help others

Dedicate your time and energy to a greater cause, to solve one or some of the biggest problems of humanity, in this case *injustice*. People sometimes (a lot) born with less opportunities, with less chances to succeed, with no resources to study, eat, drink water or have a loving family. If you choose to live this way, you will help humankind to succeed in life, and make life more enjoyable to the people that live now. You could inspire others to do the same thing as you, and you'll be fighting every day for a cause, and a purpose.

### Live the present as its fullest

Travel, party, fall in love, quit when it isn't working, travel a lot more, meet new people, party a lot more, watch movies, make your own, try some funny stuff (you know what I mean) and enjoy it all, life will pass in a bit, and as you get older you can start to accumulate a lot of regrets. This way you'll have no regrets. 

Or maybe you'll do? Maybe you'll regret havingn't done something useful with your time, maybe you'll regret a lot of things, maybe more than what a normal person normally does, maybe there is another way... right?

## About the others

The others... That person that you look at on the street, your father, your mother, your cousins, all those people that aren't you, Albert Einstein, Richard Feynman, Freddy Vega, Cristiano Ronaldo, Leonardo Da Vinci, Jesus Christ, they are the others, what is their secret? Which path did they choose? Which path should we choose? 

Honestly, I don't know.

Honestly, I don't have even a single clue, just my own thoughts.

I propose, all of them and none of them, the simple idea is: *Try it*. You can have a life that consists on mastering your craft while being a nice person, raising a child, and traveling with your friends and family. Even raising a dog or a cat, while reading philosophy books seems like a good idea if that's what you like. 

Maybe you won't be remembered, maybe you would, you do what you like, you do what is meaningful to you, and you'll be happy most of the time, maybe humanity won't remember you as the person who  helped finishing the hungry in Africa, but your friends will remember you as the person who gave them a pizza when you got your first job. 

The point is that even when you have a normal job, in a normal neighborhood in a normal city, you are here. See what matters the more to you, and fight for it, think of what you like, and do it once in a while, hug your parents if you have them, hang out with people you like, write your thoughts, read what others have thought before you, and hopefully you'll be fine. With your chosen path you'll be making the world a better place.

## What about his future plans?

He is very disciplined, practical and interesting person (all my cousins are), I didn't know all his plans, but I'm sure a child wasn't yet on that list, travelling to Germany, learning about Economy, get a better job, even buying a PS5 were on that list, does a child represent the new priority in his life? Does a child change all his dreams? 

Well, yes and no, obviously the child became his priority above everything else, but this isn't bad necessarily, with a child under his cares,  he won't desire travel to Germany, he **will go** to Germany, he **will find** the time to learn Economy, he won't desire a better job, he **will work** day and night to get a better job, the PS5, em... Probably he won't buy a PS5, but he will play football, baseball, ride a bike, do boxing, all with his child, or maybe not, but his child would. A child makes it more difficult, but I'm sure he can.

> To my cousin, you have reminded all of us the cycle of life, you make us think and reconsider things that we were secure about. I hope to be with you and the little child that's on the way. For the next one hundred years, it's sure you'll be remembered.
>
